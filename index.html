Privacy Policy

App Name: Cheering Rider (Delivery Partner App)

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Official Website: https://www.cheeringshop.online/

Welcome to Cheering Rider. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our delivery partner mobile application collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you register as a rider, accept delivery assignments, and complete deliveries through the Cheering platform.

1. Information We Collect

To operate the delivery service and manage rider accounts, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information Provided by You

  • Account Details: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, profile photo, and authentication credentials.
  • Identity & Verification Documents: National ID, driving license, and similar documents you submit so we can verify your eligibility to work as a rider.
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle type, registration number, and related details required for delivery assignments.
  • Payout Information: Mobile wallet or bank account reference details used to pay your earnings. We do not store full card numbers or PINs.
  • Delivery Records: Accepted, completed, and cancelled orders, delivery status updates, earnings history, and proof-of-delivery data.
  • Support Communications: Messages, complaints, and feedback you send to our support team.

B. Automatically Collected Information & App Permissions

  • Location Data (including background location): With your permission, we collect your device's precise location while you are online and during active deliveries, including when the app is running in the background. This is used to assign nearby orders, provide navigation, show live delivery tracking to customers and vendors, calculate delivery distance, and prevent fraud. Location is not collected when you are offline or have logged out of duty mode.
  • Camera & Photos: Used to capture your profile photo, upload verification documents, and take proof-of-delivery photos where applicable.
  • Device & Log Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, network state, and app crash logs.
  • Push Notifications: Device tokens used to send new order requests, assignment updates, payout notices, and important announcements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data to operate, improve, and secure our services, specifically to:

  • Create, verify, and manage your rider account.
  • Assign orders and enable live tracking of deliveries.
  • Provide navigation and calculate delivery distance and fees.
  • Calculate, process, and record your earnings and payouts.
  • Send order alerts and important account notifications.
  • Detect and prevent security issues, fraud, and abusive activities.
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and safety.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is shared strictly under the following conditions:

  • Customers: While you are delivering an order, the customer can see your name, contact number, and live location to track their delivery.
  • Vendors & Stores: Vendors may see your name and contact details to hand over the order to you.
  • Service Providers: Verified infrastructure providers, SMS gateways, mapping services, and secure payment processing services operating under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by legal mandates, law enforcement, or applicable judicial processes.

4. Payment Security

Cheering Rider does not store sensitive payment credentials (such as card numbers or mobile banking PINs) on our systems. Payouts are routed through certified and secure payment channels.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile, verification, and delivery records as long as your account remains active or as needed to comply with operational, tax, and legal requirements.

Account & Data Deletion: You can request permanent deletion of your rider account along with your associated personal records at any time. You may submit this request through the in-app profile settings or by sending an email directly to cheering.app.bd@gmail.com with the subject line "Rider Account Deletion Request". Certain records may be retained where required by law.

6. Age Requirement

Cheering Rider is intended only for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If such data is identified, it is promptly removed from our databases.

7. Data Security Measures

We apply standard security protocols, including HTTPS encryption, restricted server access, and secure authentication tokens to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, modification, or exposure.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, technical upgrades, or statutory requirements. Changes become effective upon publication on this page.
